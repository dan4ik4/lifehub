## Product behavior

- A **Месяц в цифрах** panel appears in Finance under the existing month/currency selectors, for Pro or an active trial. Current-month calculations use completed local-calendar days through yesterday; today's entries join tomorrow. Past months use their entire period. Future months are rejected.
- Exact recorded income/expense/net flow, expense categories/shares, category budgets and prior-month expense comparison. Period dates and missing-data limitations are visible. Net flow is not a bank balance. Currencies are never converted or combined. Existing overview label changed from account-like balance wording to **Разница за месяц**.
- Forecast requires the user to confirm a complete recording period within the current month, ending yesterday, with at least seven days and some recorded expenses. It does not infer tracking completeness from transaction dates or pretend missing days mean zero spending.
- Remaining expense = expense in confirmed window / number of confirmed calendar days × remaining calendar days (including today). Recorded current-month expenses + that scenario are shown as **внесённое + прогноз**. If coverage began midmonth, omitted early expenses are not reconstructed and completeness before that date remains explicitly unconfirmed. No future income or account-balance forecast. Decimal arithmetic, final money rounded half-up to cents; displayed rounded daily average is not used in multiplication.
- Upcoming weekly/monthly/yearly subscription occurrences are listed as a separate count/total. They are not added to the run-rate estimate, and scheduled dates are not assumed to prove a bill remains unpaid. Uses the existing original-anchor recurrence calculation to avoid month-end drift. Debt payments, savings and investment forecasts are outside this calculation.
- Basic calculations require no AI call or assistant consent. A separate visible checkbox authorizes sending aggregate facts/category names/budget figures/subscription totals/forecast conditions to OpenAI, with existing messages/modules preferences. No transaction names, notes, account names, bank files, chat or other modules are sent.
- AI only prioritizes up to five existing fact IDs and selects an optional predefined reflection question. It cannot introduce new amounts, facts, arbitrary prose or financial actions. Unsupported IDs/questions, repeated IDs and omission of the missing-data fact reject the output. All displayed fact text and figures are generated by LifeHub, not by the model. The UI describes this behavior directly.
- One generation attempt uses one shared daily Pro AI request; reads and cached results do not. Model timeout 90 seconds, no tools, store=False, existing configured model. Empty selected periods do not call the provider. The result is a fact selection, not a free-form financial adviser.
- Limits per calculation: 10,000 transactions across selected/prior month, 300 budgets, 300 subscriptions, 100 expense categories, 60,000 characters of calculated data. Oversized input fails visibly rather than truncating analysis.

## Persistence and correctness

Migration 0017_finance_review adds one owner/month/currency cache row in finance_reviews, including source hash, attempt status and selected IDs. Source hash covers periods, coverage assumptions, timezone, calculated facts and relevant record versions. GET hides mismatched cache entries. POST rejects stale client hashes and rechecks permissions, exact data/coverage snapshot, attempt marker and account status after the provider.

Transaction/budget/subscription writes and deletes clear the owner's review cache through ordinary CRUD callbacks, including assistant executor operations. Confirmation of CSV/PDF operations uses the same invalidation. A concurrent source change or deleted reservation cannot resurrect an old result. User-row locks serialize reservations and normal relevant writes on PostgreSQL. Changing the calendar cutoff/coverage assumptions also makes old output unavailable.

Only the review can be deleted; source finance records remain. Review selections enter account exports and final purge. The cache stores selected IDs, not a second raw statement or transaction snapshot.
