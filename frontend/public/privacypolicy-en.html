Health data

Connecting health data is optional. Life Hub requests read access to the steps and sleep metrics you select in Apple Health or Health Connect. Life Hub does not write to your phone's health app.

After you confirm, daily totals for the past seven days are sent over HTTPS to lifehapp.online and saved in your account so you can view them on your phone and on the website. Raw measurements and device names are excluded. Life Hub does not send these metrics to OpenAI, use them for advertising or sell them.

Data refreshes when you open the app and when you sync manually. You can turn off sync in Health and revoke read permissions in Apple Health or Health Connect settings. Turning off sync keeps previously imported data. You can delete imported metrics separately in Health. Export and account data deletion are available in Life Hub Settings.

Metrics depend on the connected sources. Missing data indicates that no value was available. The app is intended for personal tracking.
